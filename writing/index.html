Writing

The publishing system is ready before the archive goes live.

Writing is part of the long-term structure of the portfolio, but the v1 launch keeps the archive intentionally empty until the first pieces are ready.

Current focus

Planned editorial themes

Mobile architecture and delivery

Writing will focus on the systems that keep mobile products shippable: roadmap alignment, app architecture, APIs, CI, and team coordination.

  • Grounded in real product delivery rather than abstract process.
  • Good fit for lessons that become public over time.

Apple platform engineering

Another stream will cover Swift, SwiftUI, native frameworks, and the technical decisions behind premium Apple-platform experiences.

  • Useful for long-lived codebases on iOS, watchOS, tvOS, and macOS.
  • Likely to mix implementation details with product-quality concerns.

AI-assisted engineering

A third area is agentic workflow design: where AI helps engineering teams move faster without lowering standards on privacy, correctness, or maintainability.

  • Focus stays on practical use, not hype.
  • The same rigor should apply to automation as to any other tool.
Archive

A real archive surface, ready for publication

The writing area now has an actual archive model, localized post-page support, and a dedicated empty state. It launches without public essays on purpose, but the system is no longer just placeholder copy.

Status

No essays published yet

The archive, metadata, and future article pages are already part of the site architecture. The first texts will appear here when they are ready for public release.

Context

Publishing model

Long-form essays first

The default format is thoughtful articles, not a rapid-fire notes feed. The site should favor durable pieces over constant posting.

Project and systems notes

Some entries can become case-study style notes on architecture choices, delivery patterns, or lessons from products once they are safe to publish.

Bilingual by architecture

The archive is already built on a localized content model, so publishing in English and French can follow the same static-first structure.

Approach

Post system

Localized post routes

Future essays can have dedicated English and French pages with their own metadata, alternates, and canonical routes.

  • The structure already supports localized article detail pages.
  • Language parity can be kept without hand-building duplicate templates.

Editorial metadata built in

The article system is ready for page titles, descriptions, reading-time metadata, and long-form section structure without changing the renderer later.

  • Posts can ship with the same quality bar as project pages.
  • SEO and structure are considered before content volume exists.

Static by default

The archive remains fully static and manually curated until there is a clear reason to add more complexity.

  • No CMS, no external service, and no runtime backend are required.
  • Publishing can stay calm, private, and maintainable.
Status

Ready for first publication

Post pages, metadata, and archive structure are part of the architecture. The first public essays will be added in a later content pass.
