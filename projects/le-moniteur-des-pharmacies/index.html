Product

The app had to unite several professional needs without feeling fragmented.

#

The product mixed news, magazines, education, recruitment, and reference data, which meant the application needed to remain understandable even as the information surface grew.

Professional news and reading

The app combined real-time professional news with digital magazine access in both PDF and article formats.

  • The product had to support both quick updates and deeper reading sessions.
  • Different content modes needed to coexist cleanly.

Learning and career content

Training resources, educational media, and job listings extended the product beyond information lookup into a broader professional companion.

  • The application supported recurring professional development.
  • Career and learning content added another layer to the product surface.

One professional hub

The overall challenge was to make the application feel like a unified professional tool instead of a loose collection of services.

  • Information architecture mattered as much as implementation.
  • The value came from a coherent whole, not isolated features.
Reference

Reference depth was part of the product's usefulness.

#

Pharmaceutical data, veterinary medicine references, and supporting databases had to remain accessible and practical within a mobile interface built for regular use.

Drug reference access

The application surfaced pharmaceutical data across large reference sets so pharmacists could use the product as a working tool, not just a reading destination.

  • Depth of reference content shaped the product design.
  • Practical lookup had to stay fast and reliable.

Dense information presentation

A key part of the work was presenting professional data clearly enough to remain useful on mobile even when the underlying information was complex.

  • Data presentation was a product responsibility.
  • Mobile constraints had to be respected without oversimplifying the content.

Regular-use ergonomics

Because the product was built for recurring professional consultation, navigation and content access had to support repeat usage over time.

  • The interface had to reward familiarity.
  • Efficiency mattered more than novelty.
Execution

Native implementation helped keep a broad product coherent.

#

The work was grounded in hands-on Swift development, which kept product experience, content complexity, and technical implementation closely connected.

Swift-based delivery

Native iOS implementation made it easier to keep the application responsive and to shape interactions around the realities of frequent professional use.

  • Performance and clarity supported trust in the product.
  • Native delivery improved control over the user experience.

Feature work with product awareness

Implementation was not limited to isolated screens; it required understanding how different professional needs fit together inside one application.

  • Technical work stayed tied to information design.
  • Usability was part of the engineering responsibility.

A practical professional tool

The end goal was to help pharmacists return to the app regularly for information, learning, and reference needs without friction.

  • The product needed to earn repeated use.
  • Success depended on reliability and practical value.
