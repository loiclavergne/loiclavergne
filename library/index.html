Manual shelves, ready for real books

Current shelf

This shelf is meant for the books currently in progress, with lightweight status and optional reading notes.

  • Manual curation keeps the signal high.
  • No account sync is required to make the page useful.

Recent reads

Finished books can surface as a compact editorial shelf rather than an exhaustive catalogue.

  • A few strong notes matter more than logging everything.
  • The structure supports month and year views later.

Reference shelf

Technical, product, and design references can live separately from broader reading so the library stays clear and navigable.

  • Useful for books that continue shaping engineering practice.
  • Also a good fit for recurring recommendations.

Tracking model

Year, month, now

Selective notes over exhaustive logs

The goal is not to mirror a reading service. The site should publish only the parts of the reading life that feel useful and durable.

Static-first by default

All shelves and counts can be updated manually in the content file without introducing external services, accounts, or background jobs.

Approach

Library system

Future-ready book pages

The library can support dedicated pages for books, notes, and reading context once real entries exist.

  • Localized detail pages can be added without redesigning the page architecture.
  • A single static generator still controls the archive shape.

Manual curation stays intentional

The reading area is designed for selected titles and lightweight notes rather than for mirroring a third-party tracking service.

  • The signal should stay editorial rather than exhaustive.
  • Manual control preserves privacy and long-term clarity.

Static-first reading log

Counts, current reads, and future notes remain editable in the content source with no sync jobs, accounts, or background processing.

  • The public site stays lightweight and private.
  • Reading data can evolve without creating operational complexity.
